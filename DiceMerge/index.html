Dice Merge

Drop matching dice to merge & reach the ultimate die!

How to Play

  • Tap the glowing die in the NEXT tray and, keeping your finger down, drag it onto an empty cell to drop it.
  • Three or more dice with the same number touching — including diagonally — will merge into the next value.
  • Chain merges for bonus combo score!
  • Use power-ups: Bomb clears a die, Undo reverts your last move, Cannon blasts a whole row.
  • Game ends when the board is full and no merges are possible.
